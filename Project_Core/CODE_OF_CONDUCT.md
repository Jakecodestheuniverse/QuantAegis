# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are building tools for an industry where integrity is a legal requirement, not just a value. The same standard applies to this community.

We as contributors, maintainers, and participants pledge to make participation in QuantAegis a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

**Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences — especially across the finance/tech divide
- Giving and graciously receiving constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Acknowledging domain expertise — financial professionals and technologists should learn from each other
- Citing sources and being precise, especially when discussing regulatory or financial topics

**Behaviors that are not acceptable:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately spreading financial misinformation
- Using this community or this project to facilitate market manipulation, insider trading, or other illegal activities
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Financial Integrity Standards

Given the nature of this project, we hold additional standards:

- **No MNPI** — Do not share material non-public information in any project channel (Issues, Discussions, PRs, etc.)
- **No market manipulation** — This project may not be used or discussed in the context of market manipulation strategies
- **No fabricated benchmarks** — Do not misrepresent model performance, backtest results, or comparisons with competitors
- **Disclose conflicts of interest** — If you work for a firm that would benefit from a specific design decision, say so

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces — such as at conferences, on social media, or in media coverage of the project.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at: conduct@quantaegis.dev

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

**Enforcement guidelines:**

| Severity | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Correction** | Unprofessional language, off-topic content | Private written warning |
| **Warning** | Sustained inappropriate behavior, minor harassment | Public warning + temporary interaction ban |
| **Temporary Ban** | Serious violation or pattern of behavior | Temporary ban from all community spaces |
| **Permanent Ban** | Pattern of violations, harassment, or severe single incident | Permanent ban |
